#FRONT MATTER (title, subtitle and how-to text; edit freely)
Hawaiian Topical Dictionary
Hawaiian to English, arranged by part of speech, then by category, then alphabetically.
How to use this book
Each section is a part of speech (pronouns, nouns, verbs, adjectives, and so on). Within a section,
words are grouped into categories such as Animals or Motion & Travel, and listed alphabetically.
Larger categories are divided into subcategories such as Pets & Farm Animals or Sea Life, with
words that fit none of them listed at the end under Other. The ʻokina and kahakō are ignored when
alphabetizing.
A word used as more than one part of speech is defined in each section, with a note such as [also v.].
A word that fits more than one category is listed in each, with a note such as [also in Plants].
† marks a word spelled with letters borrowed from English (such as b, d, r, s, t, or v), an older
spelling style that standard Hawaiian replaces with native letters.
